\section{Related Literature}
\label{sec:literature}

\paragraph{Internet access as a productive input.} A first literature treats connectivity as an input into production rather than a consumption amenity. \citet{hjort2019} exploit the arrival of submarine Internet cables in Africa and find increases in employment, especially in higher-skill occupations, together with evidence of firm entry, productivity, and exporting. \citet{akerman2015} use the rollout of broadband in Norway and find that broadband adoption in firms improved the labor-market outcomes and productivity of skilled workers while substituting for unskilled workers in routine tasks. Software development is a particularly connectivity-dependent form of such skilled, nonroutine work.

\paragraph{Infrastructure outages and Internet shutdowns.} A second literature studies what happens when an infrastructure input becomes unavailable. \citet{allcott2016} estimate that electricity shortages reduce the revenues and producer surplus of Indian manufacturing plants, but that productivity losses are smaller because many inputs can be stored during outages, and that plants without generators are affected more severely. Their emphasis on coping margins parallels this paper's distinction between online publication, which requires connectivity, and local code creation, which does not. Internet shutdowns are a deliberate form of such outages. \citet{aceto2018} provide a comprehensive survey of Internet outages, and \citet{schachenhofer2023} analyze how Internet blackouts affect information flows in organizations. \citet*{tagat2024} develop an econometric framework for measuring shutdown costs across countries and emphasize impacts on output, employment, and investment, and \citet{gunsilius2023} estimate reductions in economic activity from a regional Internet shutdown in India using a discontinuity-based method. These studies motivate the hypothesis that intentional connectivity losses can generate immediate real economic costs.

\paragraph{Measuring software production with GitHub data.} A third literature uses digital trace data to measure software work. \citet{nagle2019} links the use of open-source software to firm productivity, finding positive value-added returns for firms with complementary capabilities, which helps establish that open-source activity is economically meaningful production. GitHub is especially useful because public events are timestamped and tied to concrete production and collaboration tasks. \citet{wachs2022} use GitHub to map the geography of open-source software production, and \citet{birkholz2026} use GitHub collaboration data to construct a global network of software production across locations and to study software development as tradable services. \citet{hu2023} study how pandemic lockdowns affected open-source contributions in China and show that the direction of the effect can depend on context and adaptation. \citet{kreitmeir2024} compile daily GitHub outcomes to study Italy's temporary ban on ChatGPT. Their design is particularly close methodologically: because GitHub actions are relatively rare at the user-day level, they transform count outcomes into binary daily indicators and report continuous outcomes as a robustness check. I follow that logic by making the daily active-user rate the primary outcome and treating counts as secondary outcomes.

\paragraph{Comparative-case causal inference.} Synthetic control methods construct a weighted combination of untreated units that approximates the treated unit before an intervention. The approach originates in \citet{abadie2003}, who estimate the economic costs of terrorist conflict in the Basque Country relative to a synthetic comparison region, an early application to a major political disruption, and was formalized by \citet*{abadie2010}. \citet{abadie2021} emphasizes transparent donor selection, pre-treatment fit, and placebo or leave-one-out exercises as central diagnostics. Synthetic difference-in-differences combines reweighting of units and time periods with difference-in-differences structure \citep{arkhangelsky2021}. I use conventional synthetic control as the headline specification because its weights and pre-fit are transparent in this application. Finally, I use regression discontinuity in time as an additional temporal check, following the cautions in \citet{hausman2018} regarding serial correlation, functional form, and the conceptual differences between time-based and cross-sectional RD designs.
