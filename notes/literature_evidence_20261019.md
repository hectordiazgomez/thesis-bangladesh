# Literature evidence used in the advisor revision

Checked 19 October 2026. The revised section compares questions, identification,
findings and limitations rather than listing disconnected ideas.

| Source | Verified evidence | Role in the thesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hjort and Poulsen (2019), [publisher](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20161385) | Cable arrival and terrestrial network exposure; employment and firm outcomes. | Connectivity expansion is different from suddenly removing established access. |
| Akerman, Gaarder and Mogstad (2015), [author working paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20826) | Norwegian broadband rollout; skill and task complementarity. | Motivate task heterogeneity without inferring a GitHub user's occupation. |
| Allcott, Collard-Wexler and O'Connell (2016), [publisher](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20140389) | Electricity shortages, hydroelectric supply variation, storage and generator adaptation. | Motivate the distinction between local work and delayed publication. |
| Zhang and Zhu (2011), [publisher](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.101.4.1601) | Contributions also change among Wikipedia users who remain unblocked. | Explain why collaborator spillovers threaten untreated-donor assumptions. |
| McDermott and Hansen (2021), [author code and paper](https://github.com/grantmcdermott/covgit) | GitHub activity and temporal labor reallocation during COVID-19. | Distinguish timing of recorded activity from total labor input. |
| Kreitmeir and Raschky (2024), [version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01964v2) | Italy's ChatGPT ban, international GitHub comparison and heterogeneous task/experience responses. | Closest measurement precedent; a tool restriction is different from loss of network access. |
| Abadie (2021), [author PDF](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/jel.20191450.pdf) | Feasibility, donor construction, predictor balance and interpretive limits. | Explain the constrained objective and why pre-fit is a diagnostic, not proof. |
| Ferman and Pinto (2021), [publisher](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.3982/QE1596) | Imperfect fit can leave bias under selection on unobserved confounders. | Newly added bibliography entry and motivation for transparent holdout/fit reporting. |
| NAICS classifier, [code](https://github.com/alexanderquispe/naics-github-train), [model card](https://huggingface.co/aquiro1994/naics-github-classifier-multilingual) | Repository-text model, released multilingual checkpoint and its validation limits. | Labels are application domains, not verified employment industries. |

The new empirical numbers are generated locally from the saved data and are not
drawn from these literature sources. The original national estimates and their
result files are preserved. No new political chronology claim was introduced.
